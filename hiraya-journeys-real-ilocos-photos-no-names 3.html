The Hiraya idea

A journey built around a dream.

The name Hiraya refers to a vision, hope, or wishful thinking of the heart. Combined with “Journeys,” it expresses the goal of helping make customers’ travel dreams come true.

Hiraya Journeys was created to present the wonders of Ilocos Norte — its mesmerizing nature, rich history, unique culture and warm Ilocano hospitality — while creating meaningful opportunities for visitors to discover and appreciate the province.

Vision: To become the most trusted and beloved travel agency and a prime source of authentic, responsible and soulful journeys that celebrate Ilocano culture and empower local communities.

Mission: Developing unique tourist experiences that allow visitors to enjoy and appreciate the natural beauty, recreation opportunities, cultural heritage and history of Ilocos Norte.

“Built by five, guided by one heart.”

Sample itinerary from the plan

A 3-day Ilocos Norte journey.

The business plan includes a sample Manila–Ilocos Norte group itinerary. Actual schedules depend on traffic, weather, availability and final confirmation.

DAY 1 — Manila → Laoag
Meet-up and passenger pick-up • departure from Manila • lunch • heritage tour including the cathedral and Sinking Bell Tower.
DAY 2 — Ilocos Norte exploration
Continue the planned attractions, activities, meals and accommodation arrangements in the selected package.
DAY 3 — Burgos → Laoag → Manila
Kapurpurawan Rock Formation • local souvenir shopping • group lunch • free time • final briefing • departure to Manila • estimated arrival and final drop-off.
